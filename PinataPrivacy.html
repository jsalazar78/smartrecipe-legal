MyPinata Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 13, 2025

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how MyPinata (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the MyPinata mobile application (the “App”).

MyPinata is used by brands, agencies, and field teams to create, schedule, and manage product sampling events (for example, food and beverage sampling).

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to operate the App and support your organization’s sampling events.

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Name – used to identify users within the App (for example, staff or field reps assigned to events).
  • Email address – used for login, account management, and communication about your account or events.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device ID / app instance identifier – technical identifiers used to keep your session secure and help the app function correctly.

2.3 Event Data

  • Event details – such as event name, date, time, location, and products being sampled.
  • Basic status or notes related to the sampling event, if your organization chooses to record them.

Event data is linked to your organization’s account and is used to help manage and track sampling activities.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above only for:

  • Providing and operating the App and its core features.
  • Creating and managing sampling events and assignments.
  • Authenticating users and securing access to the App.
  • Responding to support requests and troubleshooting issues.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

4. Firebase and Data Storage

MyPinata uses Google Firebase as its primary backend service to store and manage data.

Firebase may process the following information on our behalf:

  • Name and email address you use in the App.
  • Device or app identifiers needed for app functionality.
  • Event and project data created or managed within the App.

Firebase acts as a data processor/service provider, and we use it only to support the internal operation of the App (for example, storing user accounts and event details).

For more details on Firebase’s own privacy and security practices, please refer to Google’s official Firebase documentation and privacy materials.

5. Data Sharing

We may share your information only in the following limited situations:

  • Service providers – such as Firebase, solely to help us operate the App.
  • Legal requirements – if required by law, regulation, or valid legal request.
  • With your organization – event and user information may be visible to your employer, client, or program administrator that provided your access to MyPinata.

We do not sell personal information and do not share it with third parties for their own marketing or advertising.

6. Data Retention

We keep your information only for as long as needed to operate the App and support your organization’s programs, or as required by law.

Your organization may decide how long to keep event and account data. If your account is deactivated, some information may remain in backups for a limited time.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information stored in Firebase and within the App. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children’s Privacy

MyPinata is intended for use by adults and authorized staff in professional or organizational contexts. It is not directed to children under the age required by local law, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, update, or request deletion of your personal information.

In most cases, these requests can be initiated by contacting your organization’s administrator. You may also contact us directly using the details below, and we will work with your organization to handle the request when appropriate.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes are posted means you accept those changes.
